# Authentic museum statue scans
Minneapolis Institute of Art (artsmia). CC0, verified in Objaverse metadata before download.

- 10th C CE Thai Seated Buddha: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/31857e30bf9249f788aed2dfe5eb6673
- Seated bodhisattva Guanyin, 12th C CE: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/2c865894453743a49f8e82d57e4f0a8d

Public source mirrors and original SHA256: SOURCES.json. Modified for game use: orientation, origin, uniform 1m normalization, low-detail decimation. Original surface texture/patina retained. These are scans of Thai/Chinese historical works, not reproductions of modern Vietnamese temple statues. Fifteen placements reuse these two source figures; they are not fifteen distinct deities. No primitive sacred anatomy or AI restoration.
